Privacy Policy for vita-luna

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

vita-luna is an open-source, non-commercial homebrew client for the PlayStation Vita designed to stream cloud games via Amazon Luna.

1. Data Collection & Processing

No Personal Data Collection: vita-luna does not collect, track, store, or transmit any personal identification, telemetry, or user metrics.

Authentication is performed directly between your PlayStation Vita device and official Amazon OAuth servers (api.amazon.com). No intermediate servers, proxies, or analytics services are used.

2. Credentials & Local Token Storage

OAuth tokens received from Amazon are saved locally on your device at ux0:data/vita-luna/tokens.dat. Credentials are never sent anywhere other than official Amazon Luna streaming endpoints.

3. Open Source

The full source code of this project is public and open-source for verification and review.
